As a part of my CityGap application in May of 2025, exactly a year from now, I was asked to build a neighbourhood biography that includes 3 meaningful spaces in my neighbourhood (Kapan, Kathmandu).

In that application, unbeknownst to the realm of public space, 2 out of the 3 places that I chose were *surprise surprise* public spaces: Lakhepokhari Community Park and Didi Bahini ko Basibiyalo (Older-Younger Sister’s Community).

The first one is owned and managed by the local government — capital ‘P’ Public Space, and the other created within the community by women of the neighborhood — small ‘p’ public space.
